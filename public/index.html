How it works

Welcome! This is our simple way to share the public witnessing cart. Everything is a tap or two, it only takes a moment, and there is nothing here you can break.
1
Sign in once. Tap Sign in and choose Continue with Google. Your name fills in automatically — you just add your phone number and congregation the first time.
2
Take a cart. Tap Check Out Cart and pick one shown as available in your congregation.
3
Answer one question. The main cart displays the Free Bible Course poster. If it is a second cart at the same location, you may choose one additional poster.
4
Bring it back. Tap Return Cart and confirm. Any poster that went out with the cart is checked back in at the same time.
5
Just need a poster? Tap Check Out a Poster to take one on its own, then return it the same easy way.
Common questions
Do I need an account?
Only the first time. Signing in with Google is quickest — there is no password to remember. If you would rather use an email address and password, that works too.
How do I know what is available?
The Home screen shows how many carts are free in each congregation, before you even sign in. Every poster shows how many copies are available, and anything fully checked out is clearly marked Unavailable.
What do “available copies” mean for posters?
We own a few physical copies of each poster — usually four. When someone takes one, the number goes down by one; when it comes back, it goes up again. Everyone shares the same pool.
What do the poster sections mean?
Posters are grouped so they are easy to find: this service year’s featured posters first, in the order they appear, then Watchtower, Awake, and Special Request — each listed alphabetically.
Can I see the whole year’s schedule?
Yes — tap Featured Schedule. You can view this service year or next, and print it or save it as a PDF.
Can I change how the app looks?
Tap More › Appearance to choose a colour and switch night mode on or off. You can also add the app to your phone’s Home Screen from the browser menu.
I forgot my password.
On the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot my password — you will get a link to set a new one. If you use Google, there is nothing to remember.
Something looks wrong. Who do I tell?
Tap Report an Issue and describe what happened. It goes straight to the coordinators.
